About

Organized by nature. Creative by choice.

Hi, I'm Nautica.

I started Nautica Lee Consulting because I kept watching talented people drown in the parts of their business that had nothing to do with their talent.

The creator with a full inbox of brand offers she never got to. The boutique owner who hadn't posted in three weeks. The coach whose leads were sitting in four different apps. They didn't need another course or another tool. They needed someone to take it on.

That's what I do. I come in, set up simple systems, and then run them with care. I write in your voice, protect your brand and treat your clients the way you would. You get your time back, and your business looks more put-together than it ever has.

How I work

A few things you can count on.

Your voice, not mine

Every caption, email and reply sounds like you. I build a tone guide early so consistency is baked in.

Proactive, not reactive

You'll hear from me before you have to ask. Weekly updates, monthly reports and a heads-up on anything that needs your eyes.

Discreet and trustworthy

You're handing me your inbox and your brand. I treat access, contracts and client data with the care they deserve.

Systems that outlast me

Trackers, templates and SOPs are documented and yours to keep. Nothing lives only in my head.

Honest about scope

If something's outside what I do well, I'll say so and point you to someone who does.

Easy to work with

No jargon, no long contracts, no drama. Just steady, reliable support from someone who genuinely enjoys this work.
