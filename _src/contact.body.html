Monthly work is month to month with two weeks' notice, and projects have a one-page agreement covering scope, price and date.
Will you post and reply as me?
If you want me to. We write a one-page tone guide together in the first week, and from there you can approve everything before it goes out or only the things you care about.

Usually within one to two weeks. Setup takes about a week, and the regular work starts straight after.
