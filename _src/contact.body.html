FAQ

Common questions.

How does pricing work?

Most clients pay a monthly retainer based on the services and volume involved. Media kits, brand guides and other one-time pieces are priced per project. You get a written quote before anything starts.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Retainers are month to month with a short notice period. Projects have a simple agreement covering scope and timeline.

Which platforms and tools do you work with?

Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, Facebook, LinkedIn and YouTube on the social side. Gmail, Outlook, Mailchimp, Flodesk, Kit, Notion, Canva, Google Workspace, HoneyBook, Dubsado and more on the operations side. If you use something else, I'll learn it.

Will you post or reply as me?

Yes, if you want me to. We build a tone of voice guide together up front, and you can approve content before it goes out or hand it over fully. Your call.

How quickly can we start?

Usually within one to two weeks of signing. Setup takes about a week and the regular work starts straight after.
