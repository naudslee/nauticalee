Hi, I'm Nautica.

I started Nautica Lee Consulting in 2024 after watching too many good creators and business owners lose their weeks to admin.

A creator with brand offers sitting unread in her inbox. A boutique owner who hadn't posted in three weeks. A coach with leads spread across four apps. None of them needed another tool. They needed someone to do the work.

So that is what I do. I set up simple trackers and templates, then run them week to week. I write in your voice and treat your clients the way you would.
