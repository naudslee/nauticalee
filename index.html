How it goes

  1. You email me or fill in the form. I reply within one business day.

  2. We talk for about 30 minutes about your business and what you want off your list.

  3. I send a short written quote. Monthly work is month to month. Projects have a set price and date.

  4. The first week is setup: logins, a one-page tone guide and trackers. After that it just runs. You get a weekly update and a monthly report.
